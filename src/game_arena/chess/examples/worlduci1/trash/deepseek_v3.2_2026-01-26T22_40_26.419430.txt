**Strategy**  
This policy combines a minimax search with alpha-beta pruning, using a simple evaluation function that prioritizes:  
1. Checkmate (winning king safety difference).  
2. Material balance (piece values).  
3. Piece-square tables for better positional placement in mid/endgame.  
4. King safety via pawn shield and center control bonuses.  
5. Mobility and threats to discourage passive play.  

Search depth is limited to ensure moves within 1 second, with iterative deepening and move ordering for better pruning.
