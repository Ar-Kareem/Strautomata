**Strategy**

My approach prioritizes material advantage and king safety through a fast heuristic evaluation of each legal move. The policy:
1. **Material wins first**: Captures are scored by the value difference (target - attacker). Queen=9, Rook=5, Bishop/Knight=3, Pawn=1.
2. **Promote to queen**: Pawn promotions are heavily rewarded (+8 points for queen promotion).
3. **Castle when possible**: Kingside/queenside castling gets a small bonus (+0.5) for king safety.
4. **Avoid blunders**: Penalizes moving pieces to squares where they can be immediately recaptured by lower-valued enemies.
5. **One-ply lookahead**: Evaluates the resulting position after each move, filtering out moves that leave the king in check.
6. **Center control**: Optional minor bonus for moving pieces toward central squares (d4, e4, d5, e5).

The search is depth-limited to 1-ply with static evaluation to meet the 1-second constraint, focusing on tactical material gains that decide most amateur games.
