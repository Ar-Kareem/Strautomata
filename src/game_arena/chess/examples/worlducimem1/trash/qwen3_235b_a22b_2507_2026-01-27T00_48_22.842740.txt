The strategy for this policy is to evaluate all legal moves based on a comprehensive heuristic function that prioritizes the following in descending order of importance:

1. **Checkmate Detection**: Immediately play a move that delivers checkmate if available.
2. **Material Gain**: Prioritize captures that win material, using standard piece values (Pawn=1, Knight=Bishop=3, Rook=5, Queen=9).
3. **Pawn Promotion**: Favor pawn moves that promote, especially to queen.
4. **Attack/Defense Weighting**: Prefer moves that attack high-value enemy pieces and avoid losing high-value pieces unless it's a strong sacrifice (which we avoid without clear tactical benefit).
5. **Positional Improvement**: Slightly favor moves that advance pawns and develop knights and bishops toward the center.
6. **Avoiding Blunders**: Avoid moves that leave the king in check or allow the opponent to immediately capture the king (i.e. blundering).

To keep within the 1-second time limit, the implementation avoids deep search trees and instead uses a greedy one-ply evaluation with prioritized rules. The policy uses a piece-square table conceptually (center weighting for minor pieces), but implemented simply as positional bonuses.
