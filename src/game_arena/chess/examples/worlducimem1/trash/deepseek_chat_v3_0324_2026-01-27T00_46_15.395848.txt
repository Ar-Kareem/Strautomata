Here's a chess policy that implements a basic evaluation function with material counting, piece-square tables, and simple move ordering. It prioritizes checkmates, material gains, and controlling the center while maintaining basic king safety.

Strategy:
1. First check for any immediate checkmate moves
2. Evaluate captures based on material gain/loss
3. Evaluate non-capturing moves using piece-square tables that encourage central control and king safety
4. Prioritize pawn promotions to queens
5. For equal moves, prefer moves that increase piece activity
6. Basic move ordering to consider better moves first
