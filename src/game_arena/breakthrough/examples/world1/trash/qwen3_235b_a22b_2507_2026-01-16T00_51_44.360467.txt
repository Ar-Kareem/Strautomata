The strategy for this policy is to implement a greedy heuristic-based agent that evaluates moves based on several key objectives in Breakthrough:

1. **Forward Progress**: Prioritize moving pieces closer to the opponent's home row, especially avoiding leaving pieces idle.
2. **Capturing Opponent Pieces**: Prefer captures that remove opponent pieces, especially if they open paths forward.
3. **Avoiding Pointless Moves**: Avoid moving a piece backward or sideways (which is not allowed anyway), and prefer forward advancement.
4. **Winning Condition Check**: Always check if any move leads to an immediate win (reaching opponent's home row), and prioritize it.
5. **Center Control**: Prefer moves toward the center columns (column 3 and 4) to maintain board control.
6. **Threat Evaluation**: Avoid moves that place a piece in a position where it can be captured on the next turn, when possible.

The policy generates all legal moves and ranks them using a hand-crafted evaluation function that scores each move based on:
- Whether it wins the game
- Whether it captures an opponent piece
- How much it advances the piece toward the opponent’s home row
- Whether the destination is in the center
- Whether the source and destination are safe from immediate capture

The move with the highest score is selected. This ensures strong forward pressure and efficient use of each move.
