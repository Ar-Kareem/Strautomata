**Strategy Overview**

The policy follows a simple yet effective evaluated‑move approach:

1. **Generate every legal move** for each of our pieces  
   * forward one step (only if the square is empty)  
   * diagonal forward left/right (empty – normal move, or occupied by an opponent – capture)

2. **Score each move** with a heuristic that favours winning and safe advancement:  
   * **Immediate win** – moving onto the opponent’s home row gets a large bonus and is chosen instantly.  
   * **Capture** – taking an opponent piece is valuable, but only if after the capture the landing square is not immediately vulnerable.  
   * **Progress** – the fewer rows that remain to reach the goal, the higher the score.  
   * **Safety** – a move is penalised if the destination can be captured by the opponent on their next turn (i.e., an opponent piece is positioned one step behind and one column aside).  

3. **Select the move with the highest score**.  
   If several moves share the best score, the first one found is returned (deterministic enough for the arena).
