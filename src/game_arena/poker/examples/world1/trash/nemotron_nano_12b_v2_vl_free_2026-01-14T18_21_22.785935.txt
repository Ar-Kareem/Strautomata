To solve this problem, we need to create a policy for a Poker game using a pre-defined API function `policy(state: dict) -> str` that returns the optimal move based on the current game state. The goal is to maximize the chip return in a zero-sum game, which means we need to make decisions that maximize our long-term gain.

### Approach
1. **Hand Evaluation**: The first step is to evaluate the strength of our current hand. This involves checking for various poker hands such as high cards, pairs, flushes, straights, etc., and returning a rank that signifies the strength of the hand.
2. **Pre-flop Strategy**: Pre-flop decisions are based on our hole cards. We raise if we have a strong pair (Ace, King, Queen, Jack), fold if both cards are low, and call otherwise.
3. **Post-flop Strategy**: Post-flop, we evaluate our current hand strength. If we have a strong hand (e.g., a pair or better), we call. If we have a flush or straight draw, we consider the pot odds and decide to call or raise if it is profitable.
4. **Pot Odds and Equity Calculation**: We calculate the pot odds and compare them against our hand's equity to decide whether to call or fold.

1. **Hand Evaluation**: The `evaluate_hand` function checks for various poker hands and returns a tuple representing the hand's strength. This includes straight flushes, four of a kind, full house, flushes, straights, pairs, and high cards.

This approach balances between aggressive and conservative play based on the strength of our current hand and the pot odds, aiming to maximize our long-term chip gains.
