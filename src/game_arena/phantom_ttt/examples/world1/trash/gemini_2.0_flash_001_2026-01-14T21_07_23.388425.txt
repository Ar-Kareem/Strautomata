The strategy for this policy is as follows:

1. **Win Check:** First, check if there's a winning move. Iterate through `legal_moves` and simulate placing a mark there. If it leads to a win, return that move.
2. **Block Opponent Win:** Next, check if the opponent has almost won by also iterating through `legal_moves` and temporarily place the move on a copy of the board. Check if this placement blocks the opponent's potential confirmed win.
3. **Center Preference:** If the center is a legal move, take it. It's strategically advantageous.
4. **Corner Preference:** If a corner is a legal move, take it. Corners are generally better than edges.
5. **Edge Preference:** If none of the above conditions are met, choose a random edge move from the available legal moves to avoid the center and corners that are typically preferred.
6. **Random Move:** If none of the above apply, pick the first available legal move.

This strategy prioritizes winning, blocking opponent wins, and then focuses on strategically advantageous positions (center, corners, then edges) before defaulting to a random legal move.
