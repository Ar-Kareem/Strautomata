### Strategy for Clobber AI Policy

1. **Material Advantage**: First, evaluate moves based on immediate material gain (capturing opponent pieces). Prioritize moves that reduce the opponent's piece count while preserving our own.

2. **Mobility**: Consider the mobility after each move—prefer moves that leave the opponent with fewer legal moves. Calculate the opponent's possible responses and choose moves that minimize their options.

3. **Piece Centrality**: Central pieces are more valuable because they offer more mobility. Prioritize capturing with central pieces to control the board.

4. **Board Control**: Capture toward the center to limit opponent movement and maximize future options.

5. **Edge Cases**: Handle edge and corner pieces carefully, as they have fewer movement options.

6. **Efficiency**: Use numpy for efficient board manipulation and checks. Precompute legal moves and evaluate them based on the above criteria.
