**Strategy Overview**

The policy uses a classic *iterative deepening α‑β search* to look ahead as many plies as possible within the 1 s time limit.  
Key ingredients:

1. **Move generation** – all orthogonal captures for the current player are generated by scanning the 5 × 6 board.  
2. **Immediate win check** – before searching, we test whether any capture leaves the opponent without a legal move. If such a move exists it is played instantly.  
3. **Evaluation** – for non‑terminal positions the static score is  

\[
\text{score}= (\text{#your pieces} - \text{#opponent pieces})\;+\;0.1\;(\text{#your moves} - \text{#opponent moves})
\]

A large bonus (`±10⁶`) is assigned for positions where the opponent (respectively the player) has no legal move.  

4. **Search** – the algorithm performs α‑β minimax with the player to move alternating between “you” and “opponent”.
