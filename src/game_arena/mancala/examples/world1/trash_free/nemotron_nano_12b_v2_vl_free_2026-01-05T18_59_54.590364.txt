### Approach
The approach involves simulating each possible move to evaluate its potential outcome using a heuristic score. The heuristic score is designed to prioritize moves that maximize the player's store, encourage captures, and allow for additional moves (extra turns).

1. **Simulation of Stone Distribution**: For each possible move, simulate the distribution of stones from the chosen house, tracking the positions and captures.
2. **Capture Evaluation**: After simulating the move, check if a capture occurs based on the game rules. Captures are evaluated based on the number of stones captured and their impact on the score.
3. **Heuristic Score Calculation**: Evaluate the resulting state using a heuristic that considers the stones in the player's store, any captured stones, and potential future moves.
4. **Move Selection**: Choose the move with the highest heuristic score, balancing immediate gains and future potential.
