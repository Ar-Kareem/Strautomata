To solve this problem, we need to implement a move-selection policy for the game of Kalah. The goal is to choose a move that maximizes the player's chances of winning by capturing opponent's seeds and setting up favorable positions for future moves.

### Approach
1. **Identify Legal Moves**: Generate all possible legal moves where the player has seeds in any of their houses (indices 0-5).
2. **Simulate Each Move**: For each legal move, simulate the process of dropping seeds from the chosen house and determine the resulting board state.
3. **Capture Check**: After distributing seeds, check if the last seed lands in the player's own houses and if it triggers a capture. If so, calculate the seeds captured.
4. **Store Addition Check**: Determine if the last seed lands in the player's store, which allows an extra move.
5. **Rank Moves**: Prioritize moves that result in captures, especially high-value captures (seeding opponent's houses with many seeds). If moves result in the same number of captures, prioritize those that add more seeds to the player's store. If no captures are possible, prioritize moves that allow an extra move (ending in the player's store), and rank those by the resulting seeds in the store.

### Explanation
- **Legal Moves**: The function first identifies all houses with seeds, which are potential moves.
- **Simulation of Seed Distribution**: For each valid move, simulate dropping all seeds from the chosen house into subsequent positions in a clockwise manner, updating the board state accordingly.
- **Capture Check**: After simulating the move, check if the last seed lands in a player's house. If so, capture any seeds from the opponent's corresponding house, adding them to the player's store.
- **Extra Move Check**: Determine if the move allows an extra turn by checking if the last seed lands in the player's store.
- **Move Ranking**: Moves are scored based on captures, store additions, and the ability to create extra moves. The best move is selected using these scores to ensure optimal play.

This approach efficiently balances immediate gains (captures) and long-term advantages (extra moves and store optimization) to maximize the player's chances of winning within the constraints of the game rules.
