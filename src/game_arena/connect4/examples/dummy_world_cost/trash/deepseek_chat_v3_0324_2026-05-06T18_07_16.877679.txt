# Connect 4 AI Strategy

My strategy combines several proven Connect 4 techniques:

1. **Immediate Wins/Losses Check**: First check if I can win immediately, then check if I need to block an opponent's immediate win.

2. **Center Preference**: The center columns offer more winning opportunities since they're involved in more potential lines of four.

3. **Scoring Potential Threats**: Evaluate columns based on how they contribute to future threats and limit opponent opportunities:
   - Prefer moves that create multiple potential winning paths
   - Avoid moves that give the opponent immediate winning opportunities
   - Consider both offensive and defensive potential

4. **Two-Step Threat Analysis**: Look ahead one move to see if a potential move might give the opponent a winning response.

5. **Depth-Limited Minimax**: For more complex positions, use a minimax algorithm with alpha-beta pruning limited to a reasonable depth to assess move quality.

The implementation combines all these elements with efficient evaluation to stay within the time constraints while providing strong play.
