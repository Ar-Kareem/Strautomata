        The strategy prioritizes:
        1. Winning moves.
        2. Blocking opponent's winning moves.
        3. Taking the center.
        4. Taking an opposite corner if the opponent occupies a corner and the opposite is free.

        6. Taking any available side.

                    return (opp_r, opp_c)

        for r, c in corners:

    2. Blocking moves: If the opponent can win on their next turn, block that winning move.
    3. Center: Take the center square (1, 1) if it's empty.

        ValueError: If no empty cells are available on the board, indicating the game is over
                    and no valid move can be made.

    # Iterate through all empty cells. For each, temporarily place our mark '1'
    # and check if this results in a win.
