This policy focuses on prioritizing captures to maximize immediate gain, then connects to safe groups, and finally seeks strategic positions like corners.

### Strategy
1. **Capture Priority**: Check all potential moves to capture as many opponent stones as possible. A move captures a group if placing a stone there closes all liberties of the opponent's group.
2. **Territory Expansion**: If no captures are possible, select moves that connect to multiple own stones, increasing stability.
3. **Strategic Placement**: Fallback to placing stones in key areas like corners or the center if no advantageous moves are found.
