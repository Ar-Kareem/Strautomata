The policy works as follows:
1. **Board Representation**: Converts stone lists into a 19×19 grid for fast local access.
2. **Ko Detection**: Uses persistent memory to track the opponent's previous stone positions, identifying their last move to prevent immediate recapture (Ko rule violations).
3. **Legal Move Generation**: Checks all 361 intersections for legality by verifying liberties or captures using iterative flood-fill (BFS).
4. **Move Evaluation**:
   - **Capture Priority**: High reward for capturing opponent stones (1000 points per stone).
   - **Atari Defense**: Rewards saving friendly groups in atari (800 base + 50 per stone saved).
   - **Connection Bonus**: Rewards moves that connect separate friendly groups.
   - **Opening Strategy**: Early game prioritizes star points (4-4, 3-4, tengen) and central influence; later game prioritizes proximity to existing stones.
   - **Randomization**: Small random noise breaks ties between equally good moves.

The algorithm safely passes (returns `(0, 0)`) if no legal moves exist, ensuring compliance with competition rules.
