### Thinking Process

**Understanding Pentago Rules and Strategy:**
Pentago is a two-player game where players take turns placing a marble on an empty cell of a 6x6 grid and then rotating one of the four 3x3 quadrants. The goal is to create a line of five marbles in a row (horizontally, vertically, or diagonally) after the placement and rotation. The rotation affects both players' marbles in the chosen quadrant.

**Key Observations:**
1. **Legal Move Requirements:** The move must place a marble on an empty cell and rotate a quadrant (0-3) either left (L) or right (R). The rotation must be valid (i.e., the quadrant must exist and the direction must be L or R).
2. **Winning Condition:** After the placement and rotation, check if the current player (you) has five marbles in a row. If so, the move is winning.
3. **Opponent's Move:** The opponent will try to block or create their own winning move. Thus, the policy should prioritize moves that either win immediately or block the opponent's potential winning moves.
4. **Randomness:** If no immediate win or block is possible, the policy should make a random legal move to avoid predictable patterns.

**Approach:**
1. **Generate All Legal Moves:** Iterate over all empty cells (1-6, 1-6) and all possible quadrants (0-3) and directions (L, R) to generate all possible legal moves.
2. **Simulate Moves:** For each legal move, simulate the placement and rotation to see if it results in a win for the current player.
3. **Check for Opponent's Win:** If no immediate win is found, check if the opponent can win in their next move (i.e., if the opponent can place a marble and rotate to create a line of five). If so, block such moves.
4. **Random Move:** If no immediate win or block is found, choose a random legal move.

**Implementation Details:**
- Use numpy for efficient board manipulation and rotation.
- For each move, simulate the placement and rotation, then check for a win.
- To check for a win, scan all possible lines (rows, columns, diagonals) for five marbles in a row.
- If no win is found, simulate the opponent's possible moves to see if they can win, and block those moves.
- If no immediate win or block is found, return a random legal move.

**Edge Cases:**
- Ensure that the rotation is correctly applied to both players' marbles in the specified quadrant.
- Handle cases where multiple winning or blocking moves exist (prioritize randomly or by some heuristic).
