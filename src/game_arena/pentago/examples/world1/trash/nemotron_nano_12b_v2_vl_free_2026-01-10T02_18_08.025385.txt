The strategy is to first search for any immediate winning move by trying all possible placements of your marble on an empty cell, followed by rotating any quadrant in either direction. If a move leads to a 5-in-a-row for you, it's chosen. If no such move exists, a simple fallback selects the first available empty cell with a predefined quadrant rotation.
