The algorithm evaluates all possible moves by simulating placements and rotations, then selects the move that maximizes the chance of winning while minimizing the opponent's opportunities. Key steps include:

1. **Generate Moves**: For each empty cell, consider all quadrants and rotation directions.
2. **Simulate Moves**: Place your marble and rotate the specified quadrant, then check for wins.
3. **Evaluate Moves**: 
   - **Win**: Prioritize moves that win immediately.
   - **Block**: Prioritize moves that prevent opponent wins.
   - **Threat**: Prioritize moves that create 4-in-a-row threats.
4. **Select Best Move**: Choose the highest-priority move from evaluated candidates.
