**Strategy Overview**

The policy follows the official rules of English‑checkers:

1. **Mandatory captures** – if any capture is available the policy must choose a capturing move.
2. **Longest capture chain** – among all possible capture sequences the policy selects the one that removes the most opponent pieces.  
   (If several sequences have the same length, a simple safety heuristic is used.)
3. **Safety heuristic** – a destination square that can be taken by the opponent on the next turn is avoided when possible.
4. **Promotion priority** – when liz a non‑capturing move is required, men are moved forward toward the king row; kings may move in any diagonal direction.
5. **Simple move ordering** – if several ordinary moves are equally good the first safe one is taken.

The implementation builds a complete board representation, enumerates every legal capture sequence for each of our pieces (men and kings) with a depth‑first search, and finally picks the best move according to the rules above.  The function returns a tuple `((from_row, from_col), (to_row, to_col))` that represents the start square and the final landing square after the whole (possibly multi‑jump) move.
