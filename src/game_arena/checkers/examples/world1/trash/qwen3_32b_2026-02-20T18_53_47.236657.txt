The strategy for this policy is to prioritize mandatory capture moves by maximizing the number of opponent pieces captured in a single turn. If multiple capture sequences are available with the same number of captures, the one that leads to kinging the piece is preferred. When no capture is available, the policy selects the non-capturing move that advances the piece most towards the opponent's territory or results in kinging.
