The strategy for this policy is to prioritize moves that capture boxes, giving priority to those that capture the most boxes in a single turn. When no capturing moves are available, the policy avoids creating opportunities for the opponent by selecting moves that result in the fewest threatening three-edge boxes. This minimizes immediate gains for the opponent while positioning for advantageous future moves.
