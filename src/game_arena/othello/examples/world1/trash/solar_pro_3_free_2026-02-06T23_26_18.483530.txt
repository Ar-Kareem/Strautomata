The strategy for this policy is a straightforward Othello heuristic that focuses on the most decisive factors known to dominate the early‑mid game:

* **Corner moves** – occupying a corner gives a huge positional bonus (≈ 1000 points) because a corner piece can never be flipped.
* **Edge moves** – moves on the edge but not a corner provide a moderate bonus (≈ 500 points) and often improve mobility.
* **Capture flips** – each opponent disc captured yields 100 points, encouraging aggressive attacks.
* **Mobility advantage** – the difference between the opponent’s current number of legal moves and the number they would have after the move (weighted by 30) favors moves that reduce the opponent’s future options.
* **Disc‑difference** – the total number of discs after the move is considered implicitly through flips; we also give a small extra 20 points per net disc gain.
* **Stable pieces** – a lightweight stable‑piece heuristic is omitted for simplicity, but the combination of the above already yields strong play on small boards.

The move‑selection algorithm:
1. Generates all legal moves for “you” using the standard 8‑direction flipping rule.
2. If none exist → returns `"pass"`.
3. Evaluates each legal move with the weighted score above.
4. Chooses the move with the highest score; ties are broken lexicographically (favoring “a‑file” first, then lower rank) to be deterministic.

The code runs within the 1‑second limit and always returns a legal Othello move.
