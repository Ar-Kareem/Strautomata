# Strategy Explanation

For this Othello AI policy, I'll implement a strategy that combines several well-known Othello heuristics with a lookahead search:

1. **Mobility Heuristic**: Prefer moves that maximize our mobility (number of legal moves) while minimizing opponent's mobility in subsequent moves.

2. **Stability and Corner Control**: Prioritize capturing corners (a1, a8, h1, h8) and stable positions near edges, as these are strategically important in Othello.

3. **Parity**: Recognize空前数量的 corner positions and maintain parity in remaining moves.

4. **Piece Difference**: When approaching endgame, maximize disc differential.

5. **Weighted Board Evaluation**: Use a position-based scoring matrix where center squares have lower initial weights than edge and corner-adjacent squares.

6. **1-Ply Lookahead**: Evaluate each potential move by simulating the opponent's best response to ensure we don't make overly reactive moves.

The implementation uses these principles in a prioritized manner:
- First check for corner captures (highest priority)
- Then evaluate mobility impact
- Use weighted position scores就会的 to break ties
- Finally ensures we never miss a winning move or give up corners unnecessarily
