For the `place` phase, the policy scans the 3x3 sub-boards (labeled 1-4) to find an empty cell, prioritizing filling the board to establish control or block the opponent. For the `rotate` phase, it defaults to a consistent rotation (e.g., `rotate(1,clockwise)`) to advance the game state. Given that the state space is constrained, this heuristic approach ensures legality and avoids errors while allowing for the necessary interaction with the GameMaster.
