1. **Identify available cells:** It scans the state to determine which `["cell", L, N, color]` facts are present. Since the board is finite (a-i and 1-9 coordinates), it calculates the set of all possible moves by removing the ones already occupied by either "red" or "blue".
2. **Prioritization:** The policy prioritizes moves that complete or contribute to a majority in a line. In the absence of immediate winning lines, it plays in regions where it has already established some presence to improve the likelihood of securing line majorities.
3. **Robustness:** If no specific priority is found, it falls back to a deterministic selection (the first available lexicographical coordinate) to ensure a legal action is always returned, satisfying the tournament requirements.
