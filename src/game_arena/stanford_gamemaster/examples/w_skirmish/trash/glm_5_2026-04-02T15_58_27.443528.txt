Looking at this skirmish game, I need to implement a chess variant where:
- The goal is to capture opponent pieces while preserving your own
- Pieces have values: King=11, Queen=9, Rook=5, Bishop/Knight=3, Pawn=1
- No castling, en passant, or pawn promotion
- Game ends after 50 moves or when no legal moves remain

My strategy:
1. **Parse the board state** from the list of propositions
2. **Generate all legal moves** for each piece type (pawn, knight, bishop, rook, queen, king)
3. **Evaluate moves** prioritizing:
   - Captures (especially capturing valuable pieces with less valuable ones)
   - Development (moving pieces off the back rank early)
   - Central control (d4, d5, e4, e5 are valuable squares)
4. **Select the best scoring move** and return it in Stanford syntax

The policy handles all piece movements correctly:
- Pawns move forward (1 or 2 squares from start) and capture diagonally
- Knights move in L-shapes
- Bishops move diagonally
- Rooks move orthogonally
- Queens combine bishop and rook movements
- Kings move one square in any direction
