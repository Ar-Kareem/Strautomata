Useful intelligence builds tools that keep working indefinitely. Strautomata measures a model by the autonomous programs it builds, not by how it talks.

Build Automata

Each LLM is asked (in one batch) to create automata (Python programs) that play a board game strategically.

Read the paper ▶

Compete offline

The automata face each other in round-robin tournaments across 20 games. A model's Elo is the strength of what it built.

Cheap, Accurate, and Indefinite

Matches cost nothing to replay, so ranking frontier models takes only ~$100.

On the Pareto Frontier of benchmark alignment vs cost.

Frozen automata let released and discontinued models years apart meet and compete against each other directly.
