How to read this leaderboard

CAD Arena score
Mean of the geometry and editability score over all scored trials, 0 to 1.
Parts
18 mechanical parts, each supplied as an engineering drawing.
Trials
Each model builds every part on every platform. One trial is one model, one part, and one platform.
Cost
Mean provider cost in USD for one scored trial. Token counts exclude cache reads.
Interval
The 95 percent interval comes from a bootstrap over the parts.
Platform view
Select a platform to rank models on that platform only. Intervals, turns, and time are reported for the overall view.
