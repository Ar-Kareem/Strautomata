Scoring methodology

Scoring methodology8 weighted categories, 37 ranking benchmarks, and 316 display-only records.Inspect method

Each model's overall score is a normalized weighted average of category averages. Within each category, benchmark results are normalized to a common scale and combined using weights that favor harder, less-saturated evaluations.

Confidence remains separate from score. It shows how much public benchmark evidence supports a result, while display-only benchmarks stay visible for context without receiving ranking weight.

Data comes from OpenBench, official model papers, and public leaderboards. External consensus signals are bounded calibration inputs and are not exposed in exported data.
